ACME Corp Leave Policy (2024)

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. A maximum of 8 unused days may be carried forward to the next year. Leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires 5 days' prior notice through the HRMS portal.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days.

3. Parental Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

4. Remote Work
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week. Team leads must ensure at least one in-office day for team collaboration.

5. Leave Approval
All leave requests must be submitted through the HRMS portal. HR must audit leave balances every quarter, and managers must approve or reject requests within 2 working days.
