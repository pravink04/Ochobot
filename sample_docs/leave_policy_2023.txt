ACME Corp Leave Policy (2023)

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next year. Leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires 7 days' prior notice to the line manager.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of 2 or more consecutive days.

3. Parental Leave
Maternity leave is 12 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is not covered under this policy.

4. Remote Work
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

5. Leave Approval
Leave requests are submitted by email to the line manager and copied to HR.
